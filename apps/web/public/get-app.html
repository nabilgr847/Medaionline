MedAI Hospital — in your pocket

Keep your lab reports, prescriptions, appointments and medicine reminders in one place, and reach a doctor who reviews your case. Free to start.

⬇ Download for Android Use in browser

Android 6.0 or newer · about 8 MB · no Play Store needed.
iPhone? Open the site in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

How to install the APK

  1. Tap Download for Android — the file saves to your Downloads folder.
  2. Open the downloaded file; Android asks permission → allow Install unknown apps for Chrome.
  3. If Play Protect warns you, choose More details → Install anyway (the app is signed by MedAI Hospital).
  4. Sign in with the same account you use on the website.

What you get

Your reports

Upload a lab report or prescription photo and keep it private. Only doctors you share it with can open it.

Doctor review

Request a consultation; a doctor confirms the slot and writes the prescription.

Medicine reminders

Set your doses and log each one. Your adherence is tracked for you.
